# Compress

Express the information required for the document's purpose in the minimum
compatible space. Use the supplied text or the clear target already in context.

Compress may remove optional content after building the semantic inventory. It does not choose the register.

## Preservation and selection

Inventory all semantic content before classifying it as required or optional.
Keep this proportional: a short passage needs no elaborate written ledger.
For a complex document, track claims and their supporting evidence, decisions,
proposals, requirements, constraints, risks, actions, owners, and deadlines.
Preserve modality, uncertainty, assumptions, attribution, numbers, identifiers,
links, code symbols, and terminology in retained content.

Determine what is required from the document purpose and the user's explicit
constraints. Keep background, examples, qualifications, and repetition when
they support, disambiguate, or constrain a required claim. Normalise equivalent
claims so different wording does not hide duplication.

## Draft and verify

Reconstruct from retained information when that improves compression; a short
passage may need only local edits. Use the fewest sections that preserve useful
navigation. Each sentence should contribute required information or serve a
distinct navigational purpose. Do not add unsupported framing or implications.

When both modifiers are requested, apply Compress first and Laconic second.

Compare the final candidate with the source and retained inventory for both
missing information and redundancy. Check any hard limits with an appropriate
counter. Repair concrete defects and recheck the affected constraints. Stop
when preservation and compatible limits hold and no material redundancy remains;
do not keep rewriting for marginal stylistic gains. No delegation is required.

## Hard-limit conflict

If required content cannot fit, preserve it and state the conflict instead of
silently dropping it or claiming success. Return a brief notice beginning
`Constraint conflict:` and the shortest complete version you can produce.
For numeric limits, report the measured size and requested limit; do not claim
to have proved a theoretical minimum.

## Output

Return only the compressed text, except for a necessary constraint notice or
user-requested inventory, counts, or explanation.

For rationale or combined-mode questions, consult the
[Compress operation](../../FOUNDATIONS.md#compress) and
[composition rule](../../FOUNDATIONS.md#composition). The guidance above is
sufficient for routine compression.
